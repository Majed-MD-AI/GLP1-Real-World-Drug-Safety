## Question
Which pre-specified adverse events are disproportionately reported with semaglutide, liraglutide, and dulaglutide in FDA FAERS?

## Main findings
Results pending.

## What the findings may mean
A reproducible disproportionality signal can identify a drug-event pair that deserves closer safety evaluation.

## What the findings do not mean
FAERS disproportionality does **not** establish causality, incidence, absolute risk, comparative effectiveness, or a benefit-risk conclusion by itself.

## Evidence needed next
A confirmatory real-world study would ideally use longitudinal EHR, claims, or registry data with an active comparator, explicit eligibility criteria, time zero, follow-up, confounder adjustment, and sensitivity analyses.

## Relevance to regulatory science and HTA
FAERS can contribute to post-marketing signal detection and safety surveillance. For regulatory, HTA, or reimbursement decisions, spontaneous-report evidence should be integrated with stronger evidence sources rather than interpreted in isolation.
